"In Amsterdam's embrace, where canals softly gleam,
Historic gabled houses, a picturesque dream.
Tulips burst in vibrant hues, a painter's delight,
As bicycles weave through, in morning's gentle light.

A city of reflections, where past and present blend,
Every bridge a story, around each watery bend.
From Anne Frank's quiet solace to Vondelpark's green expanse,
Amsterdam, a masterpiece, a captivating dance."
