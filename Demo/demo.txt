How to Create a Cake:

1. Gather Ingredients:
   - 2 cups flour
   - 1 cup sugar
   - 1/2 cup butter
   - 2 eggs
   - 1 cup milk
   - 1 tsp baking powder
   - 1 tsp vanilla extract

2. Preheat Oven:
   - Set oven to 350°F (175°C).

3. Prepare Batter:
   - Cream butter and sugar together.
   - Add eggs one at a time, mixing well.
   - Stir in vanilla extract.
   - Combine flour and baking powder, then add to the mixture alternately with milk.

4. Bake:
   - Pour batter into a greased cake pan.
   - Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.

5. Cool and Decorate:
   - Let the cake cool before decorating or adding frosting.

Enjoy your homemade cake!
