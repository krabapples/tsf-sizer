How to Create a Cake:

1. Gather your ingredients: flour, sugar, eggs, butter, milk, baking powder, and any flavorings.
2. Preheat the oven to the required temperature (usually 350°F/175°C).
3. Mix dry ingredients (flour, sugar, baking powder) in one bowl.
4. Mix wet ingredients (eggs, melted butter, milk, flavorings) in another bowl.
5. Combine wet and dry ingredients, mixing until smooth.
6. Grease your cake tin and pour the batter in.
7. Bake for 25–35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
8. Let the cake cool, then decorate as desired.

Enjoy your homemade cake!
