How to Create a Cake:

1. Gather Ingredients:
   - 2 cups flour
   - 1 cup sugar
   - 1/2 cup butter
   - 2 eggs
   - 1 tsp baking powder
   - 1/2 cup milk
   - 1 tsp vanilla extract

2. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).

3. Cream the butter and sugar together.

4. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well.

5. Add vanilla extract.

6. Mix dry ingredients (flour and baking powder) in a separate bowl.

7. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients, alternating with milk.

8. Mix until smooth.

9. Pour batter into a greased cake pan.

10. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.

11. Let the cake cool before serving.

Enjoy your homemade cake!
